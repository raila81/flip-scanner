Pick a time window to start a scan. A scan takes about 3 to 5 minutes. Keep this tab open while it runs.

The description, website, X and Telegram links come from the coin's own listing. Anyone can write anything there, and the links can lead to scam sites. Never connect a wallet on a site you reached from this page. The utility or meme label is a rough guess from that text, not a verdict.

Paper portfolio

Pretend you put $250 into every coin the moment it passed a scan, and sold on the plan: half at 2x, the rest at 3x, all of it at -33%. Costs of 3% per round trip are taken off. Each row is a different rule for which coins to buy, so you can see which rule actually works.

Outcomes

Every coin that passes a scan is logged here with its price at that moment. "Check results" replays the price since then and asks: did the flip plan (half at 2x, rest at 3x, stop at -33%) hit 2x first, or the stop first? The record lives in this browser only. Use Export to move it to another device.
