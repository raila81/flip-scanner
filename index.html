Which rule works?

Pretend you put $250 into every coin the moment it passed a scan, then sold on the plan: half at 2x, the rest at 3x, everything at -33%. Costs of 3% per round trip are taken off. Each row is a different rule for which coins to buy.

Every trade

"Check results" replays the price since each scan with 15-minute candles. If 2x and the stop land in the same candle, the stop counts first. The record lives in this browser only. Use Export to move it to another device, and export now and then as a backup.
