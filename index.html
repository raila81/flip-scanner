Which rule works?

The paper test copies the real Jupiter orders on each card. Every coin that passes gets two paper trades of $250 ($100 for Movers, $50 for Small pools). Buy now buys at the scan price. Limit at support places the card's limit buy at support and fills only if the price comes down to it within 12 hours. Both then hold three orders with the card's stop-loss: 50% sells at 2x, 40% at 3x, and the last 10% rides a 40% trailing stop. Costs of 3% are taken off. The first rows compare the two strategies. The other rows slice the buy-now trades by rule.

What-if exits

The same trades (both strategies, since Oct 6) replayed with other ways to sell. It shows which exit plan would have made the most, before you change the real orders. Stops fill 10% worse than their price, like a fast crash. Open trades count at today's price. Under about 30 trades the numbers mean little.

Playbooks: 9 bot setups, side by side

Each playbook is a complete bot setup the real bot can copy 1:1 with Jupiter: which passes it takes, market or limit buy, the exact sell orders, a time stop, bet size, max open coins and a daily loss limit. All of them paper trade the same coins from the VPS fast scan (since Oct 6, 2026). Costs of 3% are taken off. Open trades count at today's price. A playbook earns trust only when it is green in both the older and the newer half of its trades, and under about 30 closed trades the numbers mean little.

Control group: did the filters throw away winners?

Each GitHub run also follows up to 10 coins the filters rejected, as paper "buy now" trades with the card plan. They never count in your portfolio. If a reject reason keeps doing better than the passed coins, that filter is too strict. Under about 20 coins per row the numbers mean little.

Every trade

The hourly GitHub scan replays 15-minute candles to see what each trade did. If a target and the stop land in the same candle, the stop counts first. "Check results" does the same right now from this browser. Trades from before Oct 6 keep the old plan.

Safety scan only. A pass means no traps were found, not that the price will go up. Paper money, not advice.
